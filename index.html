Hi, I'm Daniel,

I'm a Postdoctoral researcher at the GERiiCO lab (University of Lille), working on data analysis for algorithmic fairness in music streaming platforms as part of the FairMusE Horizon Europe project.

I have 10+ years of teaching experience in higher education, combined with research skills in data analysis, music information retrieval, music perception, and musical acoustics.

I conducted my doctoral research at IRCAM studying annotations of performed piano music through the musical prosody paradigm to explore structures in music performance.
