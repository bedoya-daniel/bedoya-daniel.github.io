    Hi, I'm Daniel,

    I have 10+ years of teaching experience in higher education, combined with research skills in data analysis, music information retrieval, music perception, and musical acoustics.

    I conducted my doctoral research at IRCAM studying annotations of performed piano music through the musical prosody paradigm to explore structures in music performance. I recently finished my Postdoctoral researcher contract at the GERiiCO lab (University of Lille), working on data analysis for algorithmic fairness in music streaming platforms with the Fair MusE project.
